I'm Ken.

Hello! I’m Ken. I currently work at the Law School Admission Council as a Digital Accessibility SME, where I spend my days helping teams understand how to build digital experiences that actually work for everyone. After years of specializing in accessibility, I realized I wanted to move from advising engineers to becoming one, so I’m joining this bootcamp as the next step toward transitioning into a full accessibility engineer role. I’m excited about the challenge — not just because I love solving problems, but because I want to build tools and systems that make the web more inclusive at a deeper, technical level. I live in Atlanta now, though I’m originally from Tokyo, and my home life is wonderfully chaotic in the best way: my wife, our two kids (ages 3 and 2), and our Australian Shepherd keep things lively from morning to night. When I’m not working or studying, you’ll usually find me DJing to unwind, experimenting in the kitchen, or catching up on comics. Those hobbies help me reset and stay creative, especially while balancing work, family, and this new chapter in my career. I’m genuinely excited for what’s ahead and for the chance to grow alongside others who are also pushing themselves into the next stage of their tech journey.
